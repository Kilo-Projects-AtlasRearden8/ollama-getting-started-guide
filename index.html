Local AI with Ollama

A step-by-step, printable beginner’s guide to running AI models on your own computer — no account, no paid tier, nothing leaves your machine.

What this is

Ollama is a free, open-source app that runs AI language models directly on your computer instead of on someone else’s server. Your chats stay private, work offline after you download a model once, and cost nothing — no sign-up, no credit card, no subscription.

Written in plain English by Dylan Asdale, registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). Part of the beginner-friendly AI guides published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC, alongside The Massive AI Manual and dylanasdale.github.io.

Who this is for: anyone who can install an app and type in a box. No programming experience required.

Privacy

Ollama runs locally on your hardware. Your chats and data stay on your machine; nothing is sent to Asdale Dynamics, LLC or any other company.
