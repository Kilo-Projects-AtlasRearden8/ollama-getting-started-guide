# Local AI with Ollama: A Beginner's Manual

A step-by-step, printable guide to running AI models locally on your own computer
with Ollama — no account, no paid tier, and nothing leaves your machine.

Written in plain English by **Dylan Asdale**, registered nurse turned AI author and
founder of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). This guide is part
of the beginner-friendly AI education and practical tools that Asdale Dynamics, LLC
publishes, alongside books such as *The Massive AI Manual* and the hub site
[dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

## What this guide covers

- What Ollama is and why "local-first" matters
- Installing Ollama on macOS, Linux, and Windows
- Picking your first model
- Running your first chat
- Managing models (list, remove, update)
- Free frontends that work offline
- Printable cheat sheet

Read the full manual: [docs/ollama-getting-started.md](docs/ollama-getting-started.md)

Print the one-page cheat sheet: [docs/cheat-sheet.md](docs/cheat-sheet.md)

## Who this is for

You do not need to be a programmer. If you can install an app and type in a box, you can
use this guide. It is written for nurses, authors, small-business owners, and anyone who
wants AI that stays on their own computer — never condescending, always practical.

## Privacy and safety

Ollama runs models locally on your hardware. Your chats and data stay on your machine;
nothing is sent to Asdale Dynamics, LLC or anyone else. This guide is educational
information only and is not medical advice.
